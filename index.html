First Time Here? Welcome!

This free tool plays pure tones that many people use for relaxation, meditation, and experimental wellness. Simply:

  1. Choose a preset below (nano-detox, solfeggio, etc.)
  2. Adjust the sliders to find your perfect volume balance
  3. Press START — use headphones or speakers
  4. Optionally open Calm Radio, Rock U, or Cosmic Radio for parallel listening / masking while tones play
  5. Relax and breathe — sessions auto-stop after 20 minutes for safety

No medical claims are made. Use at your own discretion and enjoy the resonance!

The Masking track is any audio file you may enjoy balancing with any of the tones. So far, I prefer MP3. You may test a .wav if you'd like. You can also use the radio drawers instead of (or alongside) a local file.
